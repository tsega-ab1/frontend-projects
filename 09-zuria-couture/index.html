Our Craft

Woven by hand, thread by thread

Every meter of tilet — the colored border pattern that defines a habesha kemis — is woven by hand on a traditional shuttle loom, the same technique passed down through generations of Ethiopian weavers.

We work directly with a small collective of weavers in Addis Ababa, paying by the finished meter rather than the hour, so quality is never rushed to meet a quota.
