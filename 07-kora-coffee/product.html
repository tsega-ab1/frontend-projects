Sidamo, Ethiopia · Washed · Light roast

Sidamo Sunrise

Bergamot Stone fruit Honey

Grown at 1,900–2,200m by smallholders in the Sidamo zone and washed within hours of picking. Bright acidity up front, softening into stone fruit and a honeyed finish as it cools.

$18.00

Origin
Bombe cooperative, Sidamo zone, Ethiopia — 118 member farms, average plot size under 2 hectares.
Brewing recommendation
Pour-over, 1:16 ratio, water at 94°C. Also holds up well as a light-roast espresso at a shorter ratio.
Shipping
Roasted to order and shipped within 48 hours. Best brewed within 6 weeks of the roast date on the bag.
