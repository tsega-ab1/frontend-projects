Coffee began here

Ethiopia is where coffee was discovered, and it's still grown the way it always has been — shaded under native trees, hand-picked cherry by cherry, on land that's often been farmed by the same family for generations.

We buy direct from cooperatives in four growing regions, paying above the going rate for lots that meet our cupping standard, and publishing what we pay.

From bean to cup

Every lot passes through the same four checkpoints before it reaches you.

Harvest

Ripe cherries are hand-picked at peak sugar content, then sorted by hand a second time at the washing station.

Processing

Washed and natural lots are processed separately on raised beds, tracked back to the individual farm.

Cupping

Our roaster cups every lot blind before it's bought — only scores above 85 make it into a KORA bag.

Roasting

Small batches, roasted within two weeks of an order shipping, so what you get is never stale.
