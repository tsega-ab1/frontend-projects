ABOUT US

Care built around every patient who walks in

SmileLife Dental Hospital has been serving Addis Ababa families for over a decade, combining modern dentistry with a genuinely comfortable patient experience.

OUR STORY

From one clinic to a full hospital

SmileLife started as a small general practice in 2013. As demand grew for specialist care, we expanded into a full dental hospital — bringing orthodontics, implant dentistry, and pediatric care under one roof, without losing the personal attention patients came to us for in the first place.

Today our team treats patients from first checkup through complex restorative work, using digital imaging and sterilization standards that meet international protocols.
